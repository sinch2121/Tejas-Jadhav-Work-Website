Art has always been an inseparable part of who I am. What began with sketching and painting grew into a lifelong engagement with theatre, writing and cinema.

Performing arts shaped my identity as an artist and my understanding of the society around me. I believe that acting, music, dance and writing give people, especially children, the freedom to explore their creativity and communicate through art.

My practice moves between the stage and the screen, bringing together storytelling, direction and collaboration with artists across Belagavi and Hubballi.

From performing as a child artist to directing original productions, theatre became the foundation of my creative practice.

I began performing at the age of 14 with a local Marathi theatre group, playing the lead in Vrukshavalli. In 2016, I wrote my first solo act and went on to develop plays, poetry, songs, short stories and street theatre.

In 2017, I directed my first independent Hindi one-act play, Jaadu Bhari Ladki, and founded Page To Stage Art Company in association with the Belagavi Theatre Association.

Since then, I have acted in, written and directed productions with local artists, building collaborative spaces for theatre across Belagavi and Hubballi. I also served as a thespian, trainer and director at the KLE Tech Drama Club for four years, conducting workshops in performing arts and filmmaking for students at BVB and KLE CBALC.

Moving from the stage to the screen opened new ways to explore stories, experiment with visual language and bring local artists together.

I directed my first short film in 2019 for an intra-college competition. In 2021, during the pandemic, I adapted my first solo act into a screenplay and filmed Mrut Jeevant, which went on to receive recognition at international film festivals.

I have assisted established local directors and worked across productions through Page To Stage and the KLE Tech Drama Club, exploring how theatre, performance and storytelling can translate into cinema.
