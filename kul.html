Kulkarni Residence

Completed during my internship at Rio Green Environ, 2024 in my 8th semester, this G+2 residential project involved documentation, drafting, and design assistance along with preparing construction drawings and understanding on-site construction details. It was my first hands-on site experience where I supervised work, coordinated with engineers, and closely worked with masons, communicating in their language to explain drawings and resolve construction queries, which gave me practical insight into the actual execution process of local architecture.
