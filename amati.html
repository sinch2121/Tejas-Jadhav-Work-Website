Amati Residence

A residential project undertaken during the 9th semester of architecture school, taken over from the column and footing stage through design development and construction. The scope included spatial planning, architectural design, site coordination, material and fixture selection, furniture, procurement, and interior design inputs. The project provided hands-on exposure to construction processes, client coordination, and translating design decisions into a built environment.
