HOW I WORK — 05 STEPS

From Concept to Completion

01

Understand

I begin by understanding your brief, context, site and requirements — listening carefully before deciding how the project should take shape.

02

Develop

I translate ideas into sketches, drawings, models and visualisations, exploring possibilities until the right direction becomes clear.

03

Refine

I develop the chosen direction through space, proportion, materials, details and practical considerations, making sure every element works together.

04

Visualise

I use drawings, 3D models and realistic visualisations to communicate the design clearly and help you experience the space before it is built.

05

Deliver

I provide the drawings, details and design inputs needed for execution, staying involved through coordination and site consultation as the project takes shape.
