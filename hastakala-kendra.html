Urban Insert

Hastakala Kendra

The Urban Insert emerges from that question. Conceived within the flood-prone fabric of Bagalkot, the project responds to a city negotiating between growth and vulnerability. Recurrent flooding, unstructured edge development, and weakened ecological systems have gradually blurred the relationship between land, water, and habitation — threatening both spatial identity and urban resilience. This proposal positions itself not as defensive infrastructure, but as a regenerative urban framework embedded within the city’s evolving morphology. Fragmented plots are restructured into a cohesive network of streets, green buffers, mixed use clusters, and water-sensitive landscapes, where public spaces function as absorptive civic grounds mediating between density and ecological flow. Within this larger strategy, this Artisans’ Community Centre operates as a socio economic anchor. Addressing the displacement and fragmentation of traditional craft communities, the intervention consolidates decentralised production into a climate-responsive courtyard typology integrating making, exhibition, and exchange. By aligning cultural infrastructure with resilient urbanism and pedestrian connectivity, the project transforms environmental vulnerability into an opportunity for economic regeneration and adaptive urban continuity. Through the Hastakala Kendra, the Urban Insert transcends development to become a reassertion of Bagalkot’s right to endure..
