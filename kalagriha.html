Architectural Thesis

Kalagriha - Cultural Arts Center

KALAGRIHA emerges from the question of how architecture can respond to the fading yet resilient cultural spirit of Belagavi. Once a vibrant hub for theatre and storytelling, the city today lacks accessible and affordable spaces that nurture artistic growth. Positioned within the evolving urban fabric, the project is conceived as a cultural anchor rather than a standalone institution, addressing the growing linguistic and cultural gaps between communities that often limit shared artistic expression. By creating a neutral platform for theatre, film-making, and performance across languages, the proposal seeks to reconnect diverse audiences through collective cultural experiences. The intervention organizes performance, rehearsal, learning, and public engagement around a network of courtyards and semi-public thresholds that extend into the city. From intimate workshops and studios to a primary theatre and open amphitheatre, the spatial strategy balances built mass and civic void while strengthening pedestrian connectivity and everyday cultural engagement. Emphasizing affordability, adaptability, and climate-responsive design, the project aims to make artistic infrastructure accessible to emerging performers and local communities alike. KALAGRIHA ultimately becomes a shared cultural ground where art bridges language, community, and the public life of the city.
