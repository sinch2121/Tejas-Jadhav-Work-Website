Internship

RB Patil Hospital

The R.B. Patil Hospital project emerged as a strategic adaptive reuse intervention — converting an old government building in Hubli into a threestorey healthcare facility serving the surrounding urban community. Envisioned as an extension of the existing hospital located opposite the site, the proposal required not only spatial reorganization but a sensitive reinterpretation of an institutional structure into a functional medical environment. The design process balanced structural constraints with healthcare efficiency, reorganizing the ground, first, and second floors to accommodate clinical spaces, circulation clarity, and service integration. Working on this project during my internship at Rio Green Environ, under the principal architect Pratima Bengeri, I was involved from the initial documentation and site surveys to drafting major plans, elevations, working drawings, electrical and plumbing layouts, and 3D visualizations. Beyond drawings, the project offered invaluable real-time exposure — It transformed theoretical knowledge into practical architectural responsibility.
